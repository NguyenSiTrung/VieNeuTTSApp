## VieNeuTTS v0.1.12 — MP3 Export + Windows Hardening

This release adds a user-choosable MP3 export alongside WAV and ships a
large Windows reliability pass, plus a repaired CUDA activation flow.

### Added

- **MP3 export:** Text, Paragraph, Batch, and Audiobook exports now offer
  MP3 in addition to WAV (WAV remains the default). The choice is
  remembered in Settings.
- **OS-aware CUDA driver upgrade guide:** Settings shows per-OS driver
  update instructions with full English translations.

### Fixed

- **Windows CUDA activation:** repaired runtime activation on Windows,
  unified the Settings readout, and hardened the setup flow.
- **Frozen-app playback:** the installed Windows build now ships the Qt
  audio plugins it needs — artifact replay and live preview work
  out of the box instead of falling back to export-only.
- **Audio device compatibility:** USB/Bluetooth headsets without Float32
  support fall back to 16-bit output instead of staying silent.
- **File-lock races:** rapid re-preview, fast replay switching, and
  render-while-playing no longer fail with WinError 32.
- **Startup polish:** no more console flash from the driver probe,
  a second launch exits cleanly instead of contending over downloads,
  and missing QML data fails with an actionable reinstall message.
- **Long paths and antivirus:** paths beyond ~260 chars, Defender-held
  files, and oversized imports are handled or refused with a clear
  message instead of crashing.
