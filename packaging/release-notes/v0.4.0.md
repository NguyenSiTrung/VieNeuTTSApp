## VieNeuTTS v0.4.0 — Video Dubbing & Subtitles Workstation, On-Device ASR/Diarization, Natural Pacing & AI Providers

v0.3.0 redesigned the five-destination shell and moved heavy I/O off the GUI
thread. This release promotes **Dubbing & Subtitles** (**Lồng tiếng & Phụ đề**)
into a **sixth top-level sidebar destination** and turns it from an `.srt`
audio renderer into a complete **video dubbing and subtitle workstation**:
import a video or `.srt`, watch and scrub the source or dubbed video inside the
app, transcribe speech with on-device **Whisper** (`whisper.cpp` /
`faster-whisper`) or a cloud **Whisper API**, separate speakers with
**Nemotron-3 GGUF diarization** (`audio.cpp`) or built-in acoustic clustering,
clone a speaker's voice in one click directly from the video, clean up,
translate, and condense cues with any **OpenAI-compatible LLM**, align dubs to
real **acoustic speech windows** with two-stage elastic fitting, polish 50-cue
windows in **Audio Studio**, and mux the dubbed video with stream-copy or
frame-accurate **ASS burned-in captions**.

Core dependencies and the frozen bundle layout are unchanged (VieNeu SDK 3.3.0,
PySide6 ≥ 6.9). Whisper GGUF models, `whisper.cpp`, Nemotron-3 Diarization
GGUF models, and `audio.cpp` are all optional and install on demand from
*Settings → Models*. Existing settings, voices, clones, books, subtitle
workspaces, and Studio projects carry over.

### Upgrade notes

- **Navigation destinations**: the sidebar now has six destinations (`create`,
  `audiobook`, `subtitles`, `voices`, `studio`, `settings`). **Create** (*Tạo
  giọng đọc*) focuses on **Compose / Document / Multiple files** so its voice
  inspector and `TransportDock` stay active across every mode, while
  **Subtitles** (*Lồng tiếng & Phụ đề*) has its own dedicated workstation and
  pinned `SubtitleActionDock`.
- **`ffmpeg` for video dubbing**: `.srt`-only audio and aligned-subtitle
  workflows need no external tools. Importing video audio or exporting a dubbed
  video uses `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` from your system `PATH`.
- **POSIX settings permissions**: `settings.json` is now written and tightened
  to owner-only permissions (`0600`) on macOS and Linux to protect stored LLM
  and Whisper API keys.

### Added — Dedicated Dubbing & Subtitles workstation (`Lồng tiếng & Phụ đề`)

- **Three-stage workflow (`Source / Prepare / Produce` — `Nguồn / Chuẩn bị /
  Xuất bản`)**: starts with a two-card drag-and-drop launcher (drop an `.srt`
  file or a `.mp4`/`.mkv`/`.mov`/`.webm`/`.avi`/`.m4v` video), advances
  automatically as your project progresses, and adapts from a single-column
  stack at 640×420 to a two-column workstation at wide widths.
- **Pinned `SubtitleActionDock`**: sits outside the page scroll view so *Render
  dubbing* (*Tạo lồng tiếng*), playback transport, the export split menu
  (WAV/MP3, aligned `.srt`, dubbed video, *Open in Studio*), live progress, and
  *Cancel* (*Hủy*) stay anchored at the bottom of the window.
- **In-app video preview panel (`SubtitleVideoPreview`)**: watch the attached
  video across *Source*, *Prepare*, and *Produce* without leaving the app:
  - **Original audio (`Âm gốc`)** plays and scrubs the source video with its
    own audio from any stage; scrubbing highlights the matching cue.
  - **Dubbed (`Lồng tiếng`)** mutes the source video player and locks the video
    frame to the dubbed track playhead.
  - **Strict audio exclusivity & unrendered cue seeking**: starting the video
    preview stops dubbed or speaker-sample playback (and vice versa), and
    clicking any cue seeks the video frame even before a dub track has been
    rendered.
  - **Live caption overlay & graceful fallback**: displays the active cue's
    speaker badge and text over the video frame, auto-collapses to a compact
    header bar below 560 px window height outside *Source*, degrades to a
    non-fatal inline notice if the host lacks a video codec, and releases file
    handles before `ffmpeg` muxing or workspace deletion so Windows never hits
    `WinError 32`.
- **Workspace lifecycle & render protection**:
  - Switch between saved subtitle workspaces, start a new project, or delete old
    workspaces from the header bar.
  - **Staged render-invalidation confirmation**: changing timing/voice policy,
    editing cues, or running ASR/AI after rendering prompts before discarding or
    marking the rendered audio stale.
  - **Explicit stale-render state (`renderStale`)**: preserves the last render's
    statistics while flagging that cues or settings changed and explaining why
    export requires a fresh render.
- **Accessibility & keyboard navigation**: Up/Down arrow keys seek across the
  cue list with automatic scroll-into-view (`PageShell.ensureFocusVisible`),
  inline cue editing uses shared `AppTextField` controls, gated actions explain
  *why* they are disabled (`disabledReason`) with a one-click jump to *Settings
  → Models*, and notices distinguish dismissible warnings from errors.

### Added — End-to-end video dubbing, ASS burn-in & full-span ducking

- **Companion discovery & zero-reencode muxing (`core/video_dub.py`)**:
  importing a video auto-discovers a companion `.srt` with the same stem (and
  vice versa). When exporting with soft subtitles or no subtitles, video
  streams copy without re-encoding (`-c:v copy`).
- **Replace or duck background audio**: choose **Replace original audio**
  (`replace`) or **Duck background audio** (`duck`, with configurable
  background gain from `-30` to `0 dB`, default `-12 dB`).
- **Full speech- and dub-span background ducking**: `duck` mode attenuates the
  source audio across the **union of each cue's original speech window and its
  dubbed audio span** (merging gaps ≤ 150 ms with 80 ms ramps), so trailing
  source dialogue never leaks through when a dubbed line finishes earlier than
  the original speaker.
- **Soft vs. burned-in subtitles (`subtitleEmbedMode`)**:
  - **Soft subtitle track (`soft`, default)**: embeds a player-toggleable track
    (`mov_text` in MP4/MOV/M4V, `srt` in MKV, `webvtt` in WebM) tagged with the
    detected/selected language code instead of `und`. `.avi` explains that soft
    tracks are unsupported and points to burn-in.
  - **Burned into video (`burn`)**: generates a frame-matched **ASS** script
    with `PlayRes` pinned to the real video dimensions, font size, margins, and
    outline scaled as fractions of the picture, a 3-line cap with adaptive font
    shrinking for long cues, bundled **Be Vietnam Pro** via `fontsdir`, literal
    escaping for `{…}`, `\`, and `-->`, and **source bit-depth preservation** so
    10-bit HDR footage stays 10-bit.
- **Streamed export progress & cancellation**: `ffmpeg -progress` updates the
  dock progress bar in real time, *Cancel* terminates `ffmpeg` cleanly mid-mux,
  and an overrun badge warns when dubbed speech extends past the video without
  truncating speech (`clamp_to_video` remains opt-in).

### Added — On-device & cloud Whisper ASR (`core/asr.py`, `core/ai_models.py`)

- **Generate subtitles from video or audio**: choose `auto`, `whisper_cpp`,
  `faster_whisper`, or `openai_api`, with source language selection, device
  picker (`auto` / `cpu` / `cuda` / `metal`), and CPU thread count (`0–32`).
- **Managed in-app downloads (*Settings → Models*)**: one-click download and
  removal with size + SHA-256 verification and throttled progress for all five
  **Whisper GGUF** models (`tiny`, `base`, `small`, `medium`, `large-v3-turbo`)
  and the staged native **`whisper.cpp` CLI runtime** (`b5454`, verified via a
  staged `--help` probe before atomic promotion).
- **Clause-aware segmentation & per-video media cache**:
  - Normalizes non-16 kHz or stereo media in-process (`soundfile` + `numpy`)
    and caches `source_16k.wav`, ASR segments, and diarization turns under
    `subtitles/_media_cache/<stem>_<hash8>/`.
  - Filters Whisper non-speech hallucinations (`[BLANK_AUDIO]`, `[MUSIC]`,
    `(applause)`, etc.), splits overlong segments at word timestamps
    (`word_timestamps=True`) and clause punctuation, and merges trailing
    micro-fragments.
  - Raises `AsrCancelled` on user cancellation so `auto` stops immediately
    instead of falling through to the next backend.
- **Hardened Cloud Whisper API (`/v1/audio/transcriptions`)**:
  - **Dedicated Whisper API settings**: configure `asr_api_base_url`,
    `asr_api_model` (default `whisper-1`, or e.g. `whisper-large-v3-turbo` on
    Groq), and `asr_api_key` separately from the chat LLM. Your LLM API key is
    never sent to a different ASR host, and `auto` only tries the cloud API
    when a Whisper API URL is explicitly set.
  - **Silence-aware 10-minute chunking**: long recordings (or float32 WAVs over
    24 MiB) split at the quietest 200 ms RMS window near each 10-minute mark,
    upload as compact 16 kHz mono PCM16 WAV chunks well under the 25 MB limit
    with a 200-character trailing `prompt` for continuity, and reassemble with
    offset segment and word timestamps.

### Added — Speaker diarization, multi-speaker cast & 1-click cloning (`core/diarization.py`)

- **Nemotron-3 GGUF & acoustic speaker diarization**: label cues (`SPEAKER_01`,
  `SPEAKER_02`, …) via **NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Diarization-GGUF** (`Q8_0` 102 MB /
  `BF16` 190 MB) on CPU or GPU through the managed **`audio.cpp` (`audiocpp_cli`
  v0.9.2)** runtime — both downloadable in-app from *Settings → Models* with
  SHA-256 verification — or fall back to the built-in zero-dependency NumPy
  acoustic clustering.
- **CPU/GPU reliability & speaker controls**:
  - Configure diarization device, thread count, and maximum speaker ceiling
    (`1–8`, merging surplus clusters by cosine similarity).
  - Forces `weight_type=f32` when the runtime resolves to CPU (2× faster than
    emulated fp16), kills hung child processes at a duration-based timeout,
    preserves ASR cues if diarization fails, remaps speaker IDs to existing
    voice assignments on re-run, and adds an optional `[SPEAKER_XX]` prefix
    toggle for `.srt` exports.
- **Multi-speaker casting & 1-click voice cloning (`SubtitleCastPanel`)**:
  - Map each speaker to any preset or cloned voice via `VoicePicker`.
  - Audition a speaker's original voice slice from the source video off the GUI
    thread.
  - Click **Clone voice** (*Tạo giọng clone*) on any speaker row to extract the
    cleanest 3.5–8.0 s single-speaker reference clip from the video and open
    *Voices → Clone* with the clip and transcript pre-populated.

### Added — OpenAI-compatible LLM subtitle pipeline & provider hardening (`core/llm_client.py`, `core/llm_refine.py`)

- **Ordered 3-step AI subtitle pipeline (`SubtitlePreparePanel`)**:
  1. **Clean ASR (`refine_asr`)**: fixes recognition typos and punctuation while
     enforcing an `allowedSpeakers` whitelist and exact cue indices.
  2. **Translate for dubbing (`translate_dub`)**: translates cues into the
     chosen target language with spoken phrasing sized to each cue's window.
  3. **Fit timing (`fit_timing`)**: condenses only cues that exceed their
     speaking budget, verifying that every accepted rewrite is shorter and fits.
- **Works with local & cloud OpenAI-compatible servers**: Ollama, LM Studio,
  `llama.cpp` server, vLLM, OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Groq, and Gemini's
  OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
  - Strips `<think>…</think>` reasoning blocks and Markdown fences, extracts the
    first JSON object via `raw_decode`, honors `Retry-After` on HTTP 429/5xx,
    and redacts API keys from all error messages.
  - **Automatic capability downgrade**: if a reasoning model (`o3`, `gpt-5`,
    `deepseek-reasoner`) or local server rejects `temperature` or
    `response_format: {"type": "json_object"}` with HTTP 400, the client
    automatically retries without the unsupported parameter and caches that
    capability per `(base_url, model)` for the session.
- **Resilient batching & parallel requests**:
  - Failed batches are isolated so completed batches are preserved and reported
    in a summary hint.
  - Truncated replies (`finish_reason="length"`) automatically bisect into
    smaller sub-batches, and any omitted cue IDs are recovered in a follow-up
    request.
  - Runs batches in parallel (`ThreadPoolExecutor`, defaulting to `1` worker on
    localhost/LAN and `3` on cloud endpoints, configurable up to `8`).
- **Advanced LLM settings & per-project glossary**:
  - Expand **Advanced LLM options** (*Tùy chọn LLM nâng cao*) in *Settings →
    Models* to set per-task model overrides (`llm_model_refine`,
    `llm_model_translate`, `llm_model_fit`), parallel request count, timeout
    (`15–600 s`), and batch size (`5–60` cues).
  - Expand **Glossary & pronouns** (*Thuật ngữ & cách xưng hô*) in the Subtitles
    *Prepare* stage to store up to 4,000 characters of character names, terms,
    and Vietnamese pronoun pairs (`project.json`), sent alongside read-only
    neighbouring cues without invalidating rendered audio.

### Added — Natural dubbing pacing & acoustic speech-window alignment (`core/align.py`, `core/audio.py`)

- **Acoustic speech bounds (`Cue.speech_start_ms` / `speech_end_ms`)**:
  separates when the source speaker actually talks from how long the subtitle
  remains on screen for reading, refined via 16 kHz RMS energy VAD
  (`refine_cues_speech_bounds`) and diarization turns, and stored sparsely in
  `project.json` so existing render caches remain valid.
- **Pre-fit vocoder silence trimming**: strips leading and trailing vocoder
  silence (`trim_speech_edges` at `-45 dBFS` with a `15 ms` breath guard and
  `5 ms` half-cosine micro-fade) before alignment so WSOLA never speeds up
  speech just to fit dead air.
- **Two-stage elastic dub fitting & silence borrowing**:
  - Anchors each cue at its speech onset (`effective_speech_start_ms +
    offset_ms`).
  - Compresses gently up to `1.15×` (`COMFORT_RATE`) first, then **borrows up to
    1,200 ms of post-cue silence** (keeping a 120 ms gap before the next cue
    when the pause is ≥ 250 ms) before compressing further toward `rate_cap`.
  - Clamps rate changes between consecutive cues from the same speaker
    (`|Δrate| ≤ 0.20×`) to avoid sudden tempo jumps.
- **Uniform merged-sentence stretching & readable caption hold**:
  - When *Merge sentences* (*Gộp câu liền mạch*) is enabled, multi-cue sentence
    units stretch once at a single uniform rate (`SubtitleTrackRenderer.add_unit_clip`)
    and split across cues weighted by clause punctuation (`split_unit_audio`).
  - Exported `.srt` cues (`adjusted_cues`) preserve readable on-screen display
    hold instead of shrinking captions to the trimmed audio duration.
- **Language- & slack-aware LLM character budgets**: `max_chars_for_window`
  scales by target language speaking rate (`14.0 cps` for Vietnamese/Latin,
  `4.8` for Chinese, `6.0` for Japanese, `5.5` for Korean, etc.) and includes
  borrowable post-cue silence.

### Added — Windowed SRT & video dubbing in Audio Studio (`core/studio.py`, `StudioTab.qml`)

- **Bounded 50-cue Studio windows**: open rendered subtitle projects in **Audio
  Studio** (`Open in Studio` from the Subtitles dock or the fourth Studio guide
  card) in 50-cue windows (`MAX_STUDIO_SRT_CUES = 50`, ranged WAV reads via
  `read_wav_range`) with window pagination and SRT-accurate inter-cue gaps.
- **Per-cue speed refitting & streaming save-back**: switch individual cues
  between *Auto-fit* and a custom speed (`0.50×–2.00×`) re-computed from the
  uncompressed `1.0×` raw audio, then click **Save back to Subtitles** (*Lưu về
  Phụ đề*) to splice the edited window back into `track.wav` in 64k-frame chunks
  and update `track.timeline.json`.
- **First-class video dubbing in Studio**:
  - `StudioClipRow` displays each clip's `[SPEAKER_XX] → <Voice>` badge and
    pre-selects that speaker's voice in the regeneration dialog.
  - Audition the source video's original audio slice for any cue directly from
    its clip row (`studioPlayOriginalCue`).
  - Click **Export dubbed video** (*Xuất video lồng tiếng*) in Studio to
    auto-commit pending FX edits, splice the active window, and mux the dubbed
    video (including soft or burned-in subtitles) in one step.
