## VieNeuTTS v0.1.16 — Studio Tab UX Refinement & Component Extraction

This release polishes the Audio Studio tab introduced in v0.1.15: a clearer
transport hierarchy, visible seek/stop controls, clickable preset chips, safer
destructive actions, precise numeric entry, and a structural refactor that
extracts the FX rack and clip rows into reusable QML components.

### Added

- **Full Transport Controls:** Visible seek-back / seek-forward (±5 s) and a
  dedicated Stop button join play/pause in the dock — actions that were
  keyboard-only (←/→, Esc) are now discoverable, with key hints in tooltips.
- **Precise Numeric Entry:** Every FX parameter (gain, speed, gap, fade) now has
  an editable numeric field beside its slider — type exact values, stepped and
  clamped to the slider's range, synced both ways.
- **Fade Dirty-State Parity:** The fade module now shows the "Chưa áp dụng" /
  "Not applied" pending hint like every other parameter, plus a live readout of
  the currently applied fade-in/fade-out values.
- **Reset Confirmation:** "Đặt lại gốc" (Reset to original) now asks before
  dropping the whole operation stack.

### Changed

- **Single Primary CTA:** The dock no longer shows two adjacent filled accent
  buttons during playback — play/pause is the one primary; quick export moved
  to an icon button beside the labeled "Xuất âm thanh…" dialog action.
- **Preset Chips Look Clickable:** All rack presets (-3 dB, 0.85×, 200 ms, …)
  render as bordered chips instead of bare text via a new `chip` button variant.
- **Safer Destructive Actions:** "Xoá vùng chọn" (cut range) and "Đặt lại gốc"
  use danger styling; the revert-to-base chip drops its misleading check icon.
- **Guide Cards Fully Clickable:** Empty-state navigation cards respond to
  clicks anywhere on the card, equalize heights, and bottom-align their CTAs.
- **Corrected Status Semantics:** The transport status dot is neutral when idle
  and accent when playing (was green when idle — an inverted convention).
- **Clip-Row Hover Feedback:** Clip rows highlight subtly on hover.
- **Consistent Hit Targets:** Icon buttons are now square with a 40 px minimum
  (was 40×32); the regen dialog's cancel reads "Hủy" instead of "Giữ nguyên".
- **Stable Busy States:** Busy buttons keep their original label while spinning
  — no more layout reflow in wrapping toolbars.

### Internal

- **Component Extraction:** `StudioTab.qml` decomposed into `StudioRackModule`,
  `StudioParamRow`, and `StudioClipRow` (1757 → 1398 lines); `AppCard` gained
  `clickable`/`cardHovered`/`cardClicked` for whole-card tap targets.
- **Localization:** English catalog regenerated for the moved/new strings;
  i18n contract test updated for the new component contexts.
- All tested `objectName` contracts preserved; 1055 tests green.
